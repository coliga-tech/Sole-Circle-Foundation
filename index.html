The Sole Circle Foundation

Bridging the digital divide, one student at a time.

We partner with communities to build and sustain technology, design, and wellness programs that give underserved youth the tools, skills, and support to thrive.

Our Mission

The Sole Circle Foundation grew from mental-health awareness gatherings hosted through Sole Play. These experiences created space for community members to discuss therapy, mindfulness, breathing, rest, and mental wellness. As the vision for community impact expanded, the Sole Circle Foundation was established to deepen the work across mental wellness, technology, creative development, and future financial-literacy programming.

Creative Technology

Day Dream

Day Dream cultivates creativity, resilience, and communication skills in young people by meeting them where they are, through sneaker design. Using technology and hands-on learning, participants develop their ideas, collaborate with instructors and peers, and use the tools we provide to create wearable sneaker prototypes.

Past partnerships with organizations such as the Atlanta Falcons, Microsoft, the Atlanta Braves, the City of Atlanta, and 100 Girls of Code have enabled us to make a significant impact on communities across Greater Atlanta through student-driven creative technology.

How to Find a Therapist

  1. Contact Your Insurance Provider

    • Request their mental health provider directory.
    • Get at least 3-4 referrals (some may not be accepting new patients).
    • Ask about copays and coverage details.

  2. Ask Your Current Healthcare Provider

    • They often know local therapists.
    • Can provide direct referrals.
    • May help coordinate care.

  3. Get Personal Recommendations

    • Ask trusted friends or family.
    • Even if their therapist isn’t available, they might suggest colleagues.
    • Request recommendations from other healthcare providers.

  4. Help Navigating the Process

    • Call the National Alliance on Mental Illness helpline at 1-800-950-6264
    • Call SAMHSA’s helpline at 1-800-662-4357
    • Watch this step-by-step guide
    • If you are struggling financially, consider talking to your therapist about it as outlined here

Wellness and Community

Fashion, Footwear, and Feelings

We host periodic events that encourage mental health awareness in our communities, from yoga and meditation classes to workshops on coping skills and journaling. We have connected hundreds of people with resources to support them on their mental health journeys.

Kicks + Cuts

For the past two years, we have sponsored Kicks + Cuts, an event that has provided more than 1,000 pairs of shoes and haircuts to the community of Dothan, Alabama.

Yoga and Gatherings

Yoga and Gatherings is a community-focused program designed to foster connection, wellness, and mindfulness through the practice of yoga. Participants of all skill levels come together to engage in guided sessions led by experienced instructors. Each gathering not only emphasizes physical postures and breathwork but also encourages social interaction and personal growth. The program aims to create a supportive environment where individuals can share experiences, establish friendships, and cultivate a sense of belonging, all while enhancing their physical and mental well-being through the art of yoga.

Sensory Santa Experience

Often, families who have children with special needs miss out on common experiences due to a lack of access. We partnered with four local schools to provide a sensory Santa experience to all of these families. We also provided gifts for all teachers in these schools. It takes a village, and we show up for our community!
